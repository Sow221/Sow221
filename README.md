### [CIF Credit Engine](https://github.com/Sow221/cif-credit-intelligencedescription)

<samp>credit-risk ML system · open source</samp>

> **Problem** — Credit-risk systems need more than a model: they need reproducible data workflows, controlled evaluation, reliable serving and monitoring.

Credit-risk engineering platform built as a **reproducible ML system**, not a notebook.

* Data pipelines orchestrated with **Dagster**
* **XGBoost** model tracked with **MLflow** and documented with model cards
* Method validated on **public Lending Club data** with an **out-of-time evaluation protocol**
* **FastAPI** serving with JWT authentication, rate limiting and PostgreSQL audit records
* Drift monitoring with PSI, Evidently, Prometheus and Grafana
* **107 tests** · **74% coverage** · CI with linting, type checking and container checks

### [CIF Credit Platform](https://github.com/Sow221/cif-credit-intelligence)

<samp>decision-support application</samp>

> **Problem** — A risk score alone does not tell a credit officer what to do or why.

Turns risk scores into **explainable, auditable lending decisions**.

* Four-way **decision engine**: approve · human review · adjust · reject
* **Cost-driven thresholds** balancing review capacity and error costs
* Per-client **SHAP** explanations exposed through the API
* **RBAC**, multi-tenant authentication and append-only audit records
* Temporal validation, anti-leakage controls and segmented evaluation
* Automated checks with **ruff, mypy and pytest**

## 04 · Architecture

The two CIF repositories form one engineering chain — from data to decision:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    D[("Credit data")] --> P["Dagster<br/>pipelines"]
    P -->|features| M["XGBoost<br/>MLflow"]
    M -->|model artifact| A["FastAPI<br/>scoring API"]
    A -->|scores + SHAP| E["Decision engine<br/>approve · review · adjust · reject"]
    E --> U["Decision interface<br/>React / TypeScript"]
    A -.->|metrics · PSI| O["Monitoring<br/>Evidently · Prometheus · Grafana"]

    subgraph ENGINE["CIF Credit Engine"]
        P
        M
        A
    end

    subgraph PLATFORM["CIF Credit Platform"]
        E
        U
    end
```

## 06 · Engineering principles

<table>
<tr>
<td width="8%" align="center" valign="top"><samp><b>01</b></samp></td>
<td valign="top"><strong>Evidence before claims</strong><br/>Validation protocols are defined before evaluation; synthetic results are clearly labelled.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="8%" align="center" valign="top"><samp><b>02</b></samp></td>
<td valign="top"><strong>Reproducible by default</strong><br/>Pipelines, dependencies and experiments are structured so results can be rebuilt and inspected.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="8%" align="center" valign="top"><samp><b>03</b></samp></td>
<td valign="top"><strong>Humans stay in the loop</strong><br/>Uncertain or high-impact decisions can be routed to human review.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="8%" align="center" valign="top"><samp><b>04</b></samp></td>
<td valign="top"><strong>Traceability is a feature</strong><br/>Decisions, model versions and access events should be observable and auditable.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="8%" align="center" valign="top"><samp><b>05</b></samp></td>
<td valign="top"><strong>Infrastructure follows requirements</strong><br/>Operational complexity is introduced when the system actually needs it.</td>
</tr>
</table>
